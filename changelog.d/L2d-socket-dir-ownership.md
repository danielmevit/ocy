- L2d-socket-dir-ownership: Hardened the per-user `/tmp/<app>-<uid>/` single-instance socket fallback: before binding or connecting, the directory is checked with `symlink_metadata` — it must be a real directory (never a symlink) owned by the current user, and its mode is repaired to 0700 only while we own it. Any failed check leaves the signal channel off without affecting start-up or the lock-file single-instance decision.
