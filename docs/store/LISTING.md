## Description* (required)
Ocy is a calm break reminder for your eyes and your screen time. Every so often a small card shows one hand-drawn eye
exercise for a few seconds, and every hour it asks you to step away for a real break. In between, it stays quiet in
the tray.

Ocy is gentle by design. It never steals your keyboard focus, never pops up on a screen you can't see, and waits for
a natural pause in your typing before it says anything. Step away from your desk and that time counts as your break.
During full-screen presentations and videos it holds breaks until you're done. In games, a break comes as a small
card in a calm moment, never mid-fight, and one click keeps any game silent.

Pick a rhythm that suits your day:

• Gentle: rare, short pauses
• Balanced: a short eye break every 25 minutes and a real break every hour
• Focused: long stretches of work, then a proper rest
• Or set every value yourself

What you get:

• Short eye exercises: blink, look far away, roll your eyes, palming and more, drawn by hand like a little flip book
• A real five-minute break every hour, with a gentle warning a minute before
• A timeline of your next two hours and today's breaks at a glance
• Light and dark themes that follow Windows
• 40 languages, written natively
• Works with screen readers such as Narrator

Good to know:

• No account, no ads. Works offline. Optional anonymous usage stats and crash reports, only with your yes
• Tiny: about 4 MB of memory while it waits
• Try it free for 15 days, then buy it once. No subscription

Ocy is a habit tool, not medical advice. If your eyes hurt or your vision changes, see an eye-care professional.

## Notes for certification
Ocy is a tray app: after install it starts in the notification area with no window. Left-click the sprout icon to
open Settings. Breaks appear on schedule; to see one immediately, right-click the tray icon and choose "Take a short
break now". It has a 15-day trial managed by the Store licence. Ocy sends nothing by default: only after the user
opts in to the anonymous usage stats in Settings (left-click the tray icon) does it send one anonymous daily summary
to PostHog (EU region). Turning the switch off stops it and deletes the random ID. After an unexpected close, a
small card asks whether to send a crash report (Send / Don't send); nothing is sent without that yes or the "Always
send" setting.
