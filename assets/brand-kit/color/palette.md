# Ocy palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jade | `#2F7D62` | Brand accent in the light theme; base of background G |
| Dark fill | `#348267` | Accent fill in the dark theme |
| Pressed | `#266951` | Pressed / active state |
| Tint | `#A3C7B8` | Small text on dark backgrounds |
| Art green | `#63A189` | Illustration accents |
| Deep jade | `#14573E` | Sprout outline and stem; text on light backgrounds |
| Character outline | `#12382A` | Character outline in the slide direction |
| Leaf | `#38A27A` | Sprout leaf fill |
| Pale | `#DCE9E4` | Character face; leaf crescents |
| Pale leaf | `#CFE3DA` | Pale leaf tint |
| Tile | `#F2F7F4` | White logo tile |
| Off-white | `#F6F7F5` | Light surfaces and light slides |

**Background G** (diagonal mint-jade sweep):
`linear-gradient(135deg, #4FA585 0%, #2F7D62 30%, #2A7259 60%, #1F5A46 100%)`

Text on G: white or near-white (`#F2F5F3`) at 28 px and up; contrast is at least 3:1 everywhere text sits on the slides
and about 5:1 on the jade core. Tokens: `tokens.css`, `tokens.json`. Swatches: `swatches.png`, `swatches.pdf`.
