# W5-site-laptop-photo

- Website "The real app": the drawn laptop + monitor is replaced by one real-looking laptop photo (AI-generated, edited
  for Ocy). The app screens are composited onto its display with a subtle glass edge, and they still crossfade per tab.
- The photo ships as responsive AVIF/WebP (800 and 1600 px, at most 48 KB). It is decorative (`alt=""`), lazy and has
  a fixed size. On the dark page only the photo is dimmed and vignetted, never the app screen.
- A hidden phone slot is ready for a future mobile mockup. The tabs still never move (re-measured at 1280/390/360 px).
