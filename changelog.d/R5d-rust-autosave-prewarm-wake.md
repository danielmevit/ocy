- Rust (`ocy-app`): fixed two R4b adaptive-scheduler regressions. Autosave now saves after 60 s of monotonic time since the last save (measured through an injectable `MonotonicTimeSource` seam) instead of counting ticks — with adaptive ticks (about 10 s working, 5 s away) the old tick counter stretched the interval to 10-20 minutes; the shutdown and event save triggers are unchanged. The scheduler now also wakes at `due - PREWARM_LEAD_TIME` when a break/warning is more than the 3 s lead time away (at most one extra wake per break), so the hidden card is pre-warmed before it is shown instead of only when a tick happens to land inside the lead window; the working wake-up budget stays ≤ 6/min away from a due moment (one extra wake per break). New tests: autosave at 10 s / 5 s / 1 s tick cadences, scheduler pre-warm wake unit tests, and a host-level test asserting the pre-warm decision precedes the show in the break-windows seam.
